**Dr. Zahra Monfared** was invited as an expert speaker for the four-day **YRC Schöntal Discussion Workshop on Dynamical Systems**, held from **August 25 to August 28, 2026**. The other team member doctoral student **Mr. Hannes Schwab** was selected as a student participant in that workshop. The workshop focuses on complex dynamical systems and features specialized expert sessions.
